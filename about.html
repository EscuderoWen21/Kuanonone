About Us

Learn more about Kuanonone Poultry Farm

Welcome to Kuanonone Poultry Farm

We are a specialized poultry farm dedicated to raising and selling healthy, high-quality chickens. Our farm focuses on providing chickens categorized by age, ensuring our customers get exactly what they need for their specific requirements.

Our Mission

To provide quality poultry with detailed care information to help both beginners and experienced farmers succeed in raising healthy chickens.

What We Offer

  • Chickens in three age categories: 4-month, 5-month, and 6-month-old & older
  • Detailed information about each chicken including breed, age, and characteristics
  • Guidance on proper care based on age group
  • Healthy, well-cared-for chickens from our farm
